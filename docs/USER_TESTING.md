# Real-User Onboarding Runbook

Use this runbook to collect the human evidence required by the submission without exposing personal or wallet secrets.

## Goal

Onboard at least 10 distinct people through the live `/onboarding` flow. Each tester should:

1. Open the live product.
2. Connect a Freighter wallet on Stellar Testnet.
3. Complete one meaningful action, preferably a subscription.
4. Copy the public transaction hash.
5. Submit the feedback form.
